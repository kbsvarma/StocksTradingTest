Different design: BTC/ETH relative-value pairs. Buy YES on one and NO on the
other with the SAME expiry. Choose direction from the difference in estimated
YES probabilities relative to the difference in market prices. Opposite bets
reduce shared-direction exposure but are NOT a guaranteed hedge or arbitrage;
both can lose. One contract per leg, one pair maximum each 15-minute cycle.

Two preexisting probability estimates: frozen Aug 13-27 rich logistic model,
and a normal diffusion estimate using completed spot bars and remaining time.
No refitting on new results. Net estimated edge floors 0c,3c,6c per pair.

fixed 12m or 6m left. Exit: settlement, after 2 minutes, or when modeled pair
edge has halved (minimum 1m holding). Family size 2*3*3*3=54.

Entry uses the worse of signal ask and next minute opening ask (3s proxy),
with 2c per-leg price cap. Exit uses the worse of signal bid and next minute

all prices to be valid before expiry. Report unfillable cycles and the
two-leg non-atomic execution limitation; historical paired quote availability
