Prospective resting-order experiment — frozen before collection, October14,2026

Question: after the SAME z/time/price/spread signal qualifies, can a short-lived
resting bid buy more cheaply without losing the savings to missed winners and
adverse selection? This is a separate paper experiment, not a live release.

Primary: signal ask minus3c, rounded toward less spending on the actual tick
ladder. Secondary: minus1c. Both request5contracts, post-only,10seconds, truncated
at the existing decision+25s deadline. Never lower a lane's execution floor or
exceed its original cap. If the discount falls below the floor, record a skip;
do not clamp it to an invented target. Thus effective improvement may exceed
the requested discount on markets with1c ticks. One opportunity per market,
using the first qualifying event actually delivered by the live runner.
No later-minute re-entry, repricing, or IOC fallback in these two variants.
This is intentional bid-only execution. The production bot's existing retries
remain unchanged and are reported separately as the actual comparison.

Both variants independently reserve the whole resting order against unchanged
5perentry/shared25contracts/$25exposure/$20daily loss including open risk, one
asset position, plus a$2cash cushion. Paper route cash is copied from a fresh
read-only live status at preparation; no money is moved. These are separate
counterfactual portfolios, not combined exposure or simultaneous financial bets.
Current lane z/price/time rules are copied, hashed and frozen. BTC originalz3;
WTI1.5/2.5; EUR/CAD adaptive1.75/2.75; otheroriginal2/premium3.

Fill modeling, fixed in advance:
1. Read a fresh public book after receiving the qualifying production event.
   Paper acceptance follows receipt by250ms. Capture all visible quantity at
   the bid as queue ahead; no cancellation credit. A marketable post-only bid
   is rejected rather than assigned a favorable instant fill.
2. Only subsequent non-block PUBLIC trade prints can fill a resting order.
   Opposite-outcome aggressive buys sell our outcome. At our bid, first consume
   the entire captured queue; below our bid, credit only printed quantity.
   Wrong direction, above-bid, pre-acceptance and expired prints never fill.
   Deduplicate trade IDs. Keep fractional partial fills; at most5in total.
   No unfilled remainder top-up. Quote touch alone is NOT a fill.
3. A later book below the lane floor requests cancellation with250ms latency;
   retain valid prints in that cancel race. Complete-minute z stays frozen,
   as in production, for the10s lifetime. There is no newly invented intraminute
   z calculation. Health/source drift cancels paper lifetimes and marks coverage
   unknown. Restarts do not reconstruct continuous orders from hindsight.
4. Query public prints through the expiration plus15s publication grace;
   only exchange timestamps inside the original lifetime are eligible.
   Pagination limited to5x1000per request cycle; truncation/errors/large book
   gaps are retained as incomplete coverage, never silently marked a proven miss.
5. Match a separate one-shot paper IOC on its first subsequent book after the
   same acceptance time, requiring all5contracts within the existing cap. This
   comparator separates some collection delay from price-discount effects.
   It is NOT a replay of the production IOC's complete multi-attempt path.

The receipt follower sees live signals only after the runner publishes them
(sometimes after live submission/reconciliation). The collector yields seconds
0–2 of each minute, caps its TOTAL public request rate at2/sec, and yields60s on
its own or observed liveHTTP429. Report source-to-paper-acceptance delays. This
can miss faster opportunities; absence of a modeled fill is not proof a timely
real resting bid could not fill. Visible queues exclude hidden liquidity and
counterfactual market response. Even modeled fills are not proven exchange fills.

Fees: capture live series fee_type and multiplier, refresh every6h. Quadratic
maker rate0, quadratic_with_maker_fees rate0.0175; taker0.07, multiplied by series
factor. Aggregate trade fee rounds up6dp and total buy cash to4dp. Fixed-price
partial fills use cumulative order-level fee rounding. Report maker model,
taker-fee stress and additional1c/contract friction. Hold all fills to official
settlement; losing and failed records never removed.

Outputs: per variant/asset/lane, qualified opportunities, attempted/skipped,
full/partial/unfilled/unknown, matched live prices, quantity-weighted improvement,
live winners missed and their lost PnL, avoided losses, paper-only fills, fees,
net and drawdown, actual-live and synchronous-IOC comparisons. Include every
live entry since start, even when its qualifying event was not captured.

Horizon:14calendar days from service start, then stop NEW opportunities and
finish settlements. First fills and first settlements are preliminary execution
evidence. Give a preliminary report at20settled comparable market opportunities.
Assess the design only with at least14days and100settled comparable opportunities;
also inspect the number of actual modeled resting fills. If inadequate at cutoff,
report insufficient evidence. Bootstrap by calendar day when sample supports it;
do not treat profitable fill-only subsets as the answer. No automatic live launch.

Isolation: public GET allowlist only; no keys, authenticated client, order endpoint
