# Microsoft investigation acceptance reference

This is a manually checked reference for evaluating generated research, not a
claim that a model report has passed. Financial periods and the saved market
reference must remain explicit. No profitability claim is made.

## The decision that needs to be answered

At the saved September 4 reference price of $499.70, does Microsoft's disclosed
demand and reinvestment trajectory support a cash-flow path that makes the price
attractive? Strong Azure growth alone cannot answer this.

## What a correct report must reconcile

1. June-quarter revenue rose 17.75%, operating income 18.30%, and reported net
   income 31.33%. Operating margin improved only 0.21 percentage points, while
   net margin improved 4.11 points. Most of that margin improvement therefore
   arose below operating profit, rather than from operating leverage.
2. Microsoft's release reports 22% net-income growth after its **OpenAI-only**
   adjustment. This is not a fully normalized recurring-earnings measure: the
   release separately discloses a $3.2 billion Anthropic investment gain and
   other discrete items. Do not conflate the $480 million current-quarter OpenAI
   gain with the $2.055 billion year-over-year swing in OpenAI effects, or the
   $4.963 billion full-year gain.
3. June-quarter operating cash flow was $55.441 billion, capex $35.802 billion,
   and free-cash-flow proxy $19.639 billion. Comparable prior FCF was $25.568
   billion. Operating cash flow grew 30.00%; capex grew 109.63%. The $5.929
   billion FCF decline reflects investment outrunning cash-generation growth.
   Positive FCF is not cash burn. Cash capex does not include every lease or
   future capital commitment.
4. FY2026 CFO minus capex is $66.987 billion. The saved reference price times
   July 23 reported shares produces a $3.711 trillion equity-value proxy and
   1.805% trailing FCF yield. This is not live market capitalization.
5. With five years of growth and 3% terminal growth, that equity proxy requires
   annual FCF growth of 27.35%, 37.62%, or 46.25% at respective discount rates of
   8%, 10%, or 12%. These are sensitivity requirements, not forecasts, revenue
   growth requirements, or evidence of investors' actual expectations. Current
   investment may depress the starting FCF; examine its prospective returns.
6. Azure grew 43% in the quarter. Annual Azure growth is a different period.
   Company gross margin is not Azure gross margin. Commercial remaining
   performance obligations are not recognized revenue or guaranteed near-term
   cash receipts; inspect duration and concentration before extrapolating.

## What makes the research useful

Explain whether the evidence supports a reinvestment-to-cash-recovery thesis,
what disclosed capacity demand and monetization support it, and what remains
unproven. Tie any valuation conclusion to an explicit business scenario. A
watch decision must identify that unresolved scenario, rather than invent a
price breakout or an arbitrary quarter-to-quarter cutoff. Do not turn a small
inventory balance or sub-one-percent EPS revisions into the central thesis.
