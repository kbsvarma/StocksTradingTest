A. SPORTS INTRAMATCH PRICE RESPONSE
New mechanism compared with previous hold-to-settlement/pre-game studies:
follow versus fade a >=10c midpoint move over 5 or 15 completed minutes.
Twelve fixed designs: 2 lookbacks x 2 directions x 3 exits (5min,15min,
8c target/8c stop/max15min). No optimizing league choice by final profit.
Existing full-game tapes, selected by data availability from a previously
volume-selected game universe. Canonical contract is lexicographically first
of exactly two outcome contracts; both YES/NO allowed. Mechanical entry:
10-90c ask, spread<=3c, completed prior10m volume>=5, complete lookback.
Decision from completed bars; buy worse of signal and subsequent opening ask,
capped at signal+2c. Exit at worse of signal bid and next opening bid, after
an exit condition is observed; never fill at an unobserved stop/target price.
Missing exit quotes keep holding until next executable quote or settlement.
One active position per physical game, cooldown5min, at most4 cycles/game.
Cash, fees, positions and outcomes follow chronological order. Charge fees
and 1c PER TRANSACTION; 2c and +60s execution are separate stresses.
Shared hypothetical $100, one contract, max25 contracts/$25 open debit,
$20 daily realized-minus-open-debit budget. No exchange margin assumption.

evaluation Oct1-Oct9. Finalists must earn positive net at1c and2c AND
>=10/day in selection. Rank worst-cost selection net/day. Later data is
evaluated only after selection receipt is frozen. If none passes, report
failure, not a least-bad rule as an edge. Existing history has been examined
for other strategies; this is new retrospective research, not pristine holdout.
